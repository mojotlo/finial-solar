Taylor Jackson

Founder

Taylor Jackson founded Finial Solar Consulting in 2019 after more than fifteen years of leading impact organizations in the energy and sustainability sector.

From 2016 to 2019, he served as Chief Operating Officer at Joule Energy, overseeing its transformation from a local New Orleans residential solar installer into a prominent utility-scale solar engineering, procurement, and construction firm and developer.

His work through Finial Solar has connected organizations of every size — from emerging ventures to major corporations — with renewable energy solutions.
